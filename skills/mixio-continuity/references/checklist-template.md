# Director's Continuity Checklist template

Use this structure for the written checklist. Tailor the items to the screenplay and intended audience experience. Keep it with the local shot plan and proposed Studio diff; after sync, use confirmed scene/shot IDs. It is a planning artifact, with no new Studio fields or tools.

## Scope and evidence

Record the screenplay/breakdown version, episode and scenes covered, final runtime, available Cast & World records and selected looks/configurations/views, references/anchors actually inspected, and evidence mode (`TEXT-ONLY` or `GROUNDED`). Describe an intentional jump or ambiguity as direction, and an unknown as an open decision.

## Sequence and scene checks

- [ ] Audience POV, information, emotional progression, and the cause of each dramatic change are legible.
- [ ] Geography, entrances/exits, cast count, location configuration, time/light, wardrobe, injury/condition, and consequential prop ownership are established where needed.
- [ ] Recurring characters, locations, props, voice assignments, and state changes remain coherent when a later scene returns to them.
- [ ] Scene handoffs explain the required time/space changes, carry sound/action where intended, and preserve or deliberately reset orientation.

## Shot and transition items

Write one row per concrete concern. A planned constraint becomes verified only when the stated evidence has been inspected; keep planning completion separate from rendered compliance.

| Item | Scene/shot or transition | Source/reference authority | Required direction or start → end state | Likely image/video failure | Coverage or corrective action | When to check and evidence/status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | Boardroom POV shot | Screenplay POV; character sheets own identity; seat map owns layout | Camera is the seated Boss; Ajay left, Karan right; three executives seated; Boss invisible | Room-reference faces replace cast; extra Boss head/reflection; table axis flips | Bind current cast refs; specify camera eye point, visibility/count and map positions | Prompt/reference preflight; inspect keyframe, video and adjacent cut |
| C02 | Prop handoff and next reaction | Source action and selected prop reference | Remote moves from Ajay's hand to Karan's; emotion follows the reveal | Prop duplicates or changes owner between cuts; reaction precedes cause | Make transfer legible in existing coverage or propose an inferred insert and listener reaction | Local shot rows, start/end states, generated motion and cut order |

Include the relevant frame size, camera side/height, axis, eyeline, movement path, first/last action state, expression, voice/dialogue timing, environmental state, screen/document content, and reflection/occlusion constraints. Check image risks in static frames and video risks through the action, including drift, morphing, disappearing objects, and changing layouts. Assign authority by attribute: a location anchor controls space and light; the selected character look controls identity and wardrobe.

## Missing coverage and editorial reserve

List missing shots/clips with their source beat, audience purpose, proposed duration, owning scene/shot, and `SCRIPTED`, `INFERRED`, or `OPEN DECISION` provenance. Consider establishing/re-establishing views, motivated angles, action causes, prop inserts, emotional alternatives, reactions/listeners, reverses, entrances/exits, and transitions only where useful. Update the shot/output ledger using the [editorial coverage rule](../../mixio-pipeline/references/editorial-coverage.md): generated video must reach at least 1.2× the intended final runtime.

## Follow-through

Keep open story decisions, reference-readiness gaps, and local corrections visible. Refresh affected items when the screenplay, coverage, look/reference, blocking, or generation route changes. Carry applicable constraints into each generation prompt/media binding and supported evaluation context. Record observed deviations against the item IDs, then propose a scoped repair for approval under the existing creative and generation-authorization gates.
